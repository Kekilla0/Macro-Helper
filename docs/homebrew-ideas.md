# Homebrew ideas

Ideas for the Homebrew settings page, kept here until the queue reaches the homebrew step. The base rules always
follow 2024 RAW; each idea below is an optional switch on top of it. Who raised it and why is noted so we can weigh
it again when we get there.

## Group travel

| # | Idea | RAW it changes | Raised by | Notes |
|---|------|----------------|-----------|-------|
| H1 | **Pace from Speed** (BUILT 2026-11-01 as the Pace from Speed switch, with H2 and H3) : the group's pace comes from its slowest walking member instead of the fixed table. | PHB Travel Pace table (Normal 3 mph / 24 miles a day, whatever the speed). | User + Claude, 2026-10-30 | Use the DMG's Special Movement formula (mph = Speed / 10, day = mph x hours, Fast x 4/3, Slow x 2/3, round down) so it stays a book formula. At 30 ft it gives Fast 32 / Slow 16 a day, not the table's 30 / 18. |
| H2 | **Land vehicles at their own speed** (BUILT, part of Pace from Speed) : a land vehicle with a speed set uses it, else the table. | PHB : land vehicles choose a pace as normal (table). | Claude, 2026-10-30 | For 2014 / third-party war machines that come with a speed. |
| H3 | **Pulled vehicles at the draft animals' pace** (BUILT, part of Pace from Speed) : a drawn vehicle moves at its slowest draft animal's speed. | PHB : land vehicles use the table; animals only set the pulling weight (5x carrying capacity, team adds). | User, 2026-10-30 | Pairs with H1. |
| H4 | **Two riders on one mount, for a shorter time** (BUILT 2026-11-02 as the Two Riders switch, weight only : no shorter gallop or day) : a mount can carry two riders, but its gallop (or its whole travel day) is shorter. | 2024 : a mount carries one rider (at least one size larger than it). | User, 2026-10-30 | Decide the cost : shorter gallop, fewer hours before forced-march saves, or both. |
| H5 | **Carried creatures slow the carrier more** (DROPPED 2026-11-01 : RAW weight covers it) : carrying a creature also counts as Heavily Encumbered (or similar) whatever the weight. | RAW : only weight against carrying capacity (over it = Speed 5 ft, so the group goes Slow). | Claude, 2026-10-30 | Only if RAW weight feels too forgiving in play. |
| H6 | **Medic on the road** (BUILT 2026-11-02 as the Medic on the Road switch : a card for the Medic's player each hour, a Healer's Kit use instead of the check; no Medic : the patient's DC 10 Constitution save, only in heavy rain or snow or Extreme Cold or Heat, unsheltered; a failure : a Loses Stable button) : a Medic job on the group sheet (not in the RAW job list); a downed (0 HP) member who travels needs a Medic; each hour moved, the Medic makes a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check or the patient loses Stable (back to Death Saving Throws). | RAW : a Stable creature makes no Death Saving Throws and regains 1 HP after 1d4 hours unless it takes damage; travel alone doesn't harm it. The Medic job itself is RAW-neutral (Stabilize is the Help action's DC 10 Medicine check). | User, 2026-10-31 | DC and fail result to settle at the homebrew step. |
| H7 | **Body weight by species** (BUILT 2026-11-02 as the Weight by Species switch) : a member with no typed or sheet weight gets its species' weight from the 2014 PHB Height and Weight tables (base weight + height roll x weight roll) instead of the size estimate. | 2024 gives no body weights; the module estimates by size (Medium 150 lb, Small 40 lb, Large 1,000 lb...). | User, 2026-10-31 | Bring the 2014 tables in at the homebrew step; roll once per creature and keep it (the GM can still type one). |
| H8 | **Build camp** (BUILT 2026-11-02 as the Rested Levels switch : points for fire, watch or Set Alarms, shelter, food and water, interruptions (an inn : its lifestyle) give -2 to +2 : -2 no Exhaustion lowered and 2 fewer Hit Point Dice, -1 one fewer, +1 Heroic Inspiration, +2 that and a Hit Point Die over the maximum) : a camp task for the setting-up hours; a good camp gives something (rest benefits, a bonus against the night's encounters, shelter from weather). | RAW has no camp building. | User, 2026-11-01 | Pick the benefit; the campTask lego takes it (finish runs after the rests). |
| H9 | **Hunt / Gather Water** : BUILT as the Camp Tasks switch (2026-11-01) : separate food and water checks, DCs by terrain, 2d6 + WIS each. | 2024 DMG Foraging finds food and water together, 1d6 + WIS each. | User, 2026-11-01 | Hunt without a ranged or thrown weapon, a damaging ranged cantrip or a Hunting Trap : Disadvantage (built 2026-11-03). Idea left : Cook (a cooked meal stretches the food). |
| H10 | **Weather changes during the day** (BUILT 2026-11-01 as the Weather Changes switch; hours from midnight; a jump rolls once, at the last) : each day, roll 1d12 and 2d12 for two hours; at each of those hours the weather is rolled again (the GM Roll's table). | 2024 DMG : the GM sets the weather (the module's Roll uses the DMG table once, by hand). | User, 2026-11-01 | Hook on the clock passing those hours (like dayEnded); a GM card each change; Undo puts it back. Hours from midnight or from dawn : to settle. |
| H11 | **Weather by season** (BUILT 2026-11-02 as the Weather by Season switch; d20 counts per season and roll, climate on the Journey card, °F that carries on day to day with a night drop and sets Extreme Cold / Heat; leaf button table) : the weather roll is weighted by the month / season. | RAW weather table has no seasons. | User, 2026-11-01 | No calendar module needed : Foundry v14's calendar gives each date a season (dnd5e already shows "Early Spring"). Needs a GM-editable weight table per season. Builds on H10. |

## How to add an idea

Add a row with the RAW it changes and who raised it. When the homebrew step starts, each row becomes a switch on the
Homebrew page (default off) or is dropped.
